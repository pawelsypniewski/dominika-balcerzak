Privacy policy

Effective from 11 September 2026. This is a translation of the Polish version, which prevails in case of any discrepancy.

In short: this website does not use cookies, does not collect visitor statistics, does not display advertising and has no forms. We do not collect any data about you through it. If you call, write or book a visit, your details go to the clinic’s reception so that we can arrange the appointment.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of personal data related to this website is Dominika Balcerzak, DVM (lek. wet.).

Postal address: Przychodnia Weterynaryjna Kochanowskiego, ul. Kochanowskiego 23, lok. H6, 01-864 Warszawa, Poland.

For matters concerning personal data, you can write to: recepcjakochanowskiego (at) kochanowskiegowet.pl or call: +48 22 150 35 00.

2. Cookies and tracking

The website does not store cookies or any other data in your browser (e.g. in browser storage). It does not use analytics or advertising tools, or social media buttons. All elements of the site — text, images, styles and script — come from its own server; the site does not load anything from third-party servers.

3. Website hosting (GitHub Pages)

The website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., based in the USA. Like any web server, it automatically records technical connection data when a page is displayed — including IP address, date and time, the address of the page opened and information about the browser. GitHub uses this data to keep the service secure and running.

The website controller has no access to these logs and does not use them to identify visitors. The rules under which GitHub processes this data (including transfers outside the European Economic Area) are described in the GitHub privacy statement.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and uninterrupted operation of the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Phone and email

When you call or write, you reach the reception of the Kochanowskiego Veterinary Clinic. The details you provide — e.g. your name, phone number, email address and information about your animal — are used to answer your question and arrange a visit (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract).

We keep this data for as long as necessary to deal with your enquiry. If a visit takes place, further rules are set out in the Terms of Veterinary Services of the Kochanowskiego Clinic (in Polish) and in the regulations on keeping veterinary medical records.

5. Online booking

The “Book a visit” and “Book online” buttons take you to the clinic’s website (kochanowskiegowet.pl, in Polish), where the booking system operates. Once there, the rules of that website and booking system apply. This website only passes on the information that you came from pawelsypniewski.github.io — without any data about you.

6. Your rights

In connection with the processing of your data, you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data erased or its processing restricted,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • data portability for the data you have provided to us,
  • lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

To exercise these rights, write to the address given in section 1. Providing your data is voluntary, but without a phone number or email address we cannot arrange a visit. We do not make automated decisions and do not profile visitors.

7. Changes to this policy

If the website starts working differently (e.g. a contact form or a map is added), we will update this policy and change the date at the top.
